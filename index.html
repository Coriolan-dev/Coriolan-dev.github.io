04 / About

I followed the systems, then the failure modes.

I’m a Paris-based security engineer working where smart contract behavior, protocol economics, and AI-assisted analysis meet.

At Trail of Bits, I lead and contribute to reviews across EVM, Solana, and TON. Before that, I founded an independent smart contract audit practice and delivered around ten engagements.

My route into security began with engineering studies at ESTP Paris and a self-taught transition into blockchain. I still bring the same habit from physics: define the units, test the boundary, and distrust an unstated assumption.
